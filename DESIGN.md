# Design System & Direction: Birthday Greeting for Sabilla Maharani

## 1. Identity & Concept
- **Subject**: Sabilla Maharani (Birthday: 1 November 2026)
- **Sender**: Gunawan Sitepu (Boyfriend)
- **Concept**: Contemporary Motion Graphic Digital Poster. 
- **Character**: Warm, modern, energetic, authentic, crafted. Feels like an editorial studio project, not a cookie-cutter AI SaaS template.

## 2. Anti-Slop Discipline
- **No generic AI purple-to-blue gradients or blurred floating radial orbs.**
- **No decorative background dot matrices or blueprint grids.**
- **No em dashes (`—`) anywhere in copy (R-02 compliant).**
- **No decorative `↗` arrows on every single card.**
- **No excessive pill capsules or uniform pill radiuses.**
- **No glassmorphism stacking (only sticky nav has subtle blur; content cards are solid and tactile).**
- **Restrained color palette (R-29 compliant):**
  - Base: Deep Obsidian `#0e1015`
  - Surface Card: Rich Charcoal `#161922`
  - High Contrast Text: Warm Chalk `#f5f4ef`
  - Secondary Text: Warm Stone `#9aa0ae`
  - Single Purposeful Accent: Energetic Poppy Vermilion `#ff4532` with warm golden amber `#f59e0b` detail.

## 3. Typography
- **Heading Display**: Space Grotesk / Inter tight track, confident scale and proportional rhythm.
- **Body & Captions**: Plus Jakarta Sans / system sans-serif for clean, effortless legibility.

## 4. Dials
- **ENERGY**: 3 (Lively, celebratory, and personal)
- **RHYTHM**: 3 (Varied pacing: hero billboard, horizontal ticker, organic photo gallery, staggered wishes, tactile envelope letter)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Smooth CSS keyframe ticker, interactive 3D card response, celebratory geometric particle burst, pleasant synth chimes)
